CopyBox Privacy Policy

Effective 6 November 2026

CopyBox lets you draw a box over text on your screen and copy it. This policy explains what CopyBox can see, what it does with it, and what it never does.

The short version

  • CopyBox does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data.
  • CopyBox has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything off your phone.
  • There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics.

What CopyBox accesses, and why

CopyBox uses Android's Accessibility service. Before you turn it on, the app explains this and asks for your agreement. The service is used only to:

  • Show the floating copy button over other apps.
  • Capture the area inside your box, and only when you tap Copy or Edit. The text in that picture is recognised on your phone using Google's on-device ML Kit text recognition. The picture is then thrown away. It is never saved or sent anywhere.
  • See which app is on screen, to hide the button on the lock screen and in CopyBox itself, and to leave out a social app's buttons and like counts when you copy a comment.

The recognised text is put on your phone's clipboard, where you control it like anything else you copy. CopyBox does not keep a history of what you copy.

What stays on your phone

CopyBox remembers where you placed the floating button and the size of your box, so they are the same next time. These settings stay on your phone and are deleted when you uninstall the app.

Third parties

Text recognition is done by Google ML Kit, which runs entirely on your device. Because CopyBox has no internet permission, neither CopyBox nor ML Kit can transmit data from the app.

Children

CopyBox is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
