CopyBox

Copy text from any app on Android.

How it works

  1. Tap the floating CopyBox button.
  2. Drag the box over the text you want and resize it to fit.
  3. Tap Copy. The text is on your clipboard, ready to paste.

Private by design

  • Text is recognised on your phone. CopyBox has no internet permission.
  • The screen is captured only when you tap Copy or Edit, and only inside your box.
  • No accounts, no ads, no tracking.
